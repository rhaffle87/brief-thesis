# Troubleshooting an Intrusion Detection Dataset: the CICIDS2017 Case Study (Engelen et al., 2021)

- **Penulis:** Gints Engelen, Vera Rimmer, Wouter Joosen
- **Institusi:** imec-DistriNet, KU Leuven
- **Tahun:** 2021
- **Venue:** 2021 IEEE Security and Privacy Workshops (SPW) / 6th International Workshop on Traffic Measurements for Cybersecurity (WTMC 2021), pp. 7–12
- **DOI:** [10.1109/SPW53761.2021.00009](https://doi.org/10.1109/SPW53761.2021.00009)
- **Status Berkas:** Tersedia di `Literatur/PDFs/Opsional/Engelen-2021-CICIDS2017-Troubleshooting.pdf`
- **Relevansi Penelitian:** Mendokumentasikan dan memvalidasi kelemahan struktural dataset CIC-IDS2017, termasuk flow berdurasi 0 detik, data duplikat, pembagian label yang tidak konsisten, serta risiko shortcut learning akibat fitur pengenal (seperti port/IP). Melengkapi temuan [[Rosay-2022-LycoSTand]] dan [[Dube-2024-Faulty-CIC-IDS2017]].
