# A Continual Learning Survey: Defying Forgetting in Classification Tasks (De Lange et al., 2021)

- **Penulis:** Matthias De Lange, Rahaf Aljundi, Marc Masana, Sarah Parisot, Xu Jia, Ales Leonardis, Gregory Slabaugh, Tinne Tuytelaars
- **Institusi:** KU Leuven, University of Oxford, Huawei Noah's Ark Lab
- **Tahun:** 2021 / 2022
- **Venue:** IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI), Vol. 44, No. 7, pp. 3366–3385
- **Identifier:** DOI: 10.1109/TPAMI.2021.3057446 / arXiv:1909.08383 [cs.CV]
- **Status Berkas:** Tersedia di `Literatur/PDFs/Opsional/DeLange-2021-CL-Survey.pdf` (1,38 MB, 29 halaman)
- **Relevansi Penelitian:** Menyajikan taksonomi komprehensif continual learning ke dalam 3 paradigma utama: *Replay Methods* (experience replay/generative), *Regularization-based Methods* (prior-focused seperti EWC, SI, MAS, dan data-focused seperti LwF), serta *Parameter Allocation Methods*. Diagram taksonomi dari paper ini diabadikan sebagai aset visual di `Aset/continual_learning_taxonomy_delange2021.png` untuk Bab 2 landasan teori CL.
